# Subby — Project Overview

## One-liner
Subby is an all-in-one operations platform for local hospitality businesses — pubs, cafés, restaurants, and small venues — combining digital menus, payments, table bookings, and loyalty into a single product.

## Problem
Independent venues today juggle 4–6 disconnected tools:
- Separate menu/QR-ordering vendors
- A POS or payment processor
- A booking widget that doesn't talk to the POS
- A stamp-card or loyalty app
- Manual spreadsheets for stock and reporting

The result: double entry, fragmented customer data, and high monthly SaaS cost relative to a single venue's revenue.

## Solution
A single platform where:
- The menu, booking page, payment flow, and loyalty stamp all live behind one customer-facing identity per venue.
- The venue operator manages everything from one dashboard.
- Customer behaviour (visit count, spend, favourites) flows back into loyalty automatically.

## Target users
- **Primary:** Owner-operators of single-site venues (pubs, cafés, small restaurants).
- **Secondary:** Small multi-site groups (2–10 locations).
- **Out of scope (v1):** National chains, dark kitchens, pure delivery-only operators.

## Why now
- Diners expect QR menus and contactless payment as default post-2020.
- SMB hospitality margin pressure is forcing tool consolidation.
- Payment providers (Stripe, Adyen, SumUp) now have well-documented APIs that make embedded payments tractable.

## Success metrics (v1)
- Venues onboarded
- Monthly active diners per venue
- Bookings completed via Subby vs. phone/walk-in
- Loyalty redemptions per active diner
- Gross payment volume (GPV)
