# Subby — Personas

## Operator personas

### Owner-operator — "Priya, Café Owner"
- Runs a 30-cover café, no manager layer.
- Currently uses Square for payments, a Google Form for bookings, paper stamp cards.
- Pain: every Sunday night reconciling tips, refunds, and bookings by hand.
- Wins with Subby: one dashboard, less double-entry, fewer subscriptions.

### Floor manager — "Ben, Pub Manager"
- Manages a 90-cover gastropub for an owner who isn't on site daily.
- Cares about: live booking view, table turn time, server accountability.
- Wins with Subby: real-time tabs, role-scoped staff logins, end-of-shift report.

### Multi-site owner — "Sara, runs 3 cafés"
- Wants consolidated reporting and a single brand surface across sites.
- Currently impossible without manual spreadsheets.
- Out of scope for v1 — informs Phase 5 design.

## Diner personas

### The regular — "Tom, weekly visitor"
- Visits one pub every Friday for 2 pints.
- Hates loyalty apps but happy to scan a QR.
- Wins: stamps accumulate automatically without installing anything.

### The planner — "Anita, family bookings"
- Books for groups of 6–10, often with kids.
- Needs: clear deposit policy, easy reschedule, dietary info on menu before arrival.
- Wins: booking + menu + payment under one account.

### The first-timer — "Marco, walks in once"
- Never returns to a specific venue.
- Doesn't want an account, doesn't care about loyalty.
- Constraint for Subby: every diner surface must work fully without account creation.
